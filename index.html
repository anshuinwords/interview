THE INCIDENT

The release note said “available now.”

The feature is actually in a staged rollout and is not yet available to the entire customer base.

“Available now for all customers.”
Correct state → “Rolling out gradually and becoming available to customers over the coming days.”
WHAT MATTERS

Don't start with the email.

The first question is not “Should we send a correction?” It is “What is the exact product state right now?”

  1. 01Confirm rollout status with Product / Engineering.
  2. 02Identify who actually received the incorrect claim.
  3. 03Decide whether the error creates customer action or confusion.
  4. 04Correct the message with the smallest necessary intervention.

DECISION TREE

What should PMM do next?

A

Is the feature already usable by the recipient?

If yes, the risk is mainly wording. If no, the customer may reasonably try to use something they cannot access.

B

Has the email created a broken expectation?

If customers are likely to look for the feature immediately, send a correction rather than silently waiting.

C

Can the audience be segmented?

Do not send the same correction to everyone if product state can be mapped to customer cohorts.

RECOMMENDED RESPONSE

Own the correction. Keep it narrow.

0–30 min

Freeze further sends. Confirm rollout state and affected cohorts with Product.

30–60 min

Assess opens/clicks and whether customers have been directed to an unavailable experience.

1–2 hrs

Draft a correction that states what was wrong, what is true, and when customers should expect access.

2+ hrs

Send to the affected audience. Route replies to Support and document the root cause.

We apologize for the confusion.

PRODUCT MARKETING · CUSTOMER COMMUNICATION
ROOT CAUSE

Fix the release-note system, not just the email.

01

Release readiness gate

Every “available now” claim needs an explicit product owner sign-off.

02

Audience eligibility

Separate global availability from phased rollout and beta access.

03

Single source of truth

Maintain a release-status field that PMM can verify before publishing.

04

Pre-send QA

Check feature state, audience, CTA, links, dates, and screenshots before scheduling.
